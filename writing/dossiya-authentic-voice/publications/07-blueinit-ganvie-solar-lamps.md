In total, 30 students from Ganvié 1 Public Primary School received small solar lamps to help improve their academic performance.

On December 20, two members of the Blueinit team participated in the distribution of small end-of-year gifts at the primary school in Ganvié.
Here’s what was distributed:

30 solar lamps,
4 medium recyclable bins,
4 small recyclable bins,
4 brooms.
We aim to promote education through access to green energy, proper hygiene tools, and climate actions instilled from a young age.

Who supported us in this wonderful first experience?
We thank Earth Guardians for playing a key role, along with other international organizations.
This is just the beginning!
