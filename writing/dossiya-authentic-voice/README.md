## Read this before reusing a claim
- A public post is evidence of what the author *said*, not independent proof of qualifications, results or externally reported counts.
- Preserve the separation between selections, completed activities and measurable outcomes.
- Do not attribute a team's accomplishments to one person.
- Do not generalize anecdotal youth concerns into facts about all African young people.
- Original French/English, emojis, and occasional non-standard grammar are intentionally preserved in the archives. Correct grammar only in **new drafts**.
- For graduate admissions, prioritize verified academic work; public posts primarily help recover authentic voice and contextual motivations.
