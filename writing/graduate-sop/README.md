# Graduate Statement of Purpose Writing Skill

A reusable SOP planning, drafting, and review workflow derived from the user's Stanford writing guidance and checked against Stanford Graduate Admissions and ICME public instructions on 2026-11-07.

## Files
- [SKILL.md](SKILL.md): general, evidence-led SOP procedure, writing principles, review checks, and output modes.
- [Stanford CME MS adapter](references/STANFORD_CME_MS.md): applicant-specific priorities, verified-versus-unverified evidence boundaries, Stanford-specific research alignment, and second-master's rationale.
- [Dossiya Authentic Voice](../dossiya-authentic-voice/SKILL.md): authoritative voice and personal-background writing constraints. **Read alongside this skill** for all Dossiya-authored text.

## How to invoke
> Use the SOP skill at `writing/graduate-sop/SKILL.md`, its Stanford CME MS adapter, and my Dossiya Authentic Voice skill. Audit my current statement against the actual Stanford portal requirements. Preserve my own research reasoning and language. Flag unsupported metrics, individual-versus-group ownership, missing program fit, and redundant paragraphs. Then provide only the revisions that improve accuracy and clarity.

Alternative mode: `PLAN`, `DRAFT`, `REVIEW`, or `REVISE`. The user's actual institutional AI-use policy controls which modes are permissible.
