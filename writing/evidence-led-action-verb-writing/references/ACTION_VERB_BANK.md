# Action Verb Bank: Semantics, Evidence and Safe Use

**Rule:** Select an action verb from the operation actually performed, not the impression the wording creates. A scientific status is attached to a particular claim, not to an entire career.

These categories consolidate the comprehensive verb list provided by the author. Some verbs occur in more than one category because meaning depends on the object, method and evidence. Verbs listed here are **available vocabulary, not automatically authorized claims**.

## 1. Scientific research and evidence extraction

Analyzed · Assessed · Audited · Characterized · Classified · Compared · Critiqued · Defined · Derived · Diagnosed · Documented · Evaluated · Examined · Extracted · Formulated · Hypothesized · Identified · Investigated · Measured · Modeled · Observed · Questioned · Reconciled · Reproduced · Researched · Reviewed · Scrutinized · Simulated · Studied · Synthesized · Tested · Verified · Validated · Quantified · Qualified · Interpreted · Estimated · Calibrated.

**Gate:** Audited requires a specified scope and audit method. Quantified requires a measurable result. Validated requires matching independent evidence. Reviewed does not imply independently reproduced. A dataset audit is not field validation.

## 2. Physics, mathematics and computation

Applied · Approximated · Assembled · Balanced · Bounded · Calculated · Computed · Constrained · Constructed · Converted · Derived · Designed · Developed · Discretized · Engineered · Estimated · Evaluated · Formulated · Implemented · Integrated · Modeled · Optimized · Parameterized · Programmed · Projected · Reconciled · Refined · Simulated · Solved · Standardized · Tested · Verified.

**Gate:** State governing equations, inputs, units, boundary conditions, solver and test evidence where relevant. An optimal design requires a proof, a certified bound or a properly qualified solver claim.

## 3. Communication, authorship and dissemination

Addressed · Advertised · Amplified · Arranged · Authored · Bridged · Briefed · Broadened · Campaigned · Canvassed · Cautioned · Challenged · Clarified · Coauthored · Collaborated · Composed · Considered · Consulted · Contacted · Conveyed · Convinced · Corresponded · Counseled · Critiqued · Defined · Directed · Discussed · Documented · Drafted · Edited · Enlisted · Explained · Expressed · Framed · Forged · Formulated · Illustrated · Incorporated · Influenced · Informed · Interacted · Involved · Lobbied · Marketed · Mediated · Negotiated · Observed · Outlined · Persuaded · Presented · Promoted · Proposed · Publicized · Questioned · Recruited · Reviewed · Scrutinized · Solicited · Spoke · Summarized · Synthesized · Wrote.

**Gate:** Coauthored requires verified authorship. Presented a poster and published a paper are different accomplishments. Influenced or convinced requires evidence of changed decisions.

## 4. Analysis, finance, projects and administration

Administered · Adjusted · Allocated · Analyzed · Appraised · Assessed · Audited · Balanced · Bought · Budgeted · Calculated · Capitalized · Closed · Computed · Corrected · Determined · Estimated · Managed · Marketed · Measured · Modeled · Planned · Prepared · Programmed · Projected · Reconciled · Reduced · Researched · Sold · Solicited · Scheduled · Secured · Tracked.

**Gate:** Cost savings or improvements require currency, time period, baseline and attribution. Modeled financial outcomes are not measured organizational savings.

## 5. Leadership, team management and decisions

Achieved · Acquired · Aligned · Administered · Analyzed · Anticipated · Appointed · Approved · Assigned · Authorized · Awarded · Blocked · Certified · Chaired · Changed · Charted · Contracted · Controlled · Converted · Coordinated · Cultivated · Decided · Decreased · Delegated · Designated · Developed · Directed · Eliminated · Enabled · Enforced · Enhanced · Established · Executed · Expanded · Facilitated · Finalized · Fostered · Generated · Guided · Handled · Headed · Hired · Hosted · Incorporated · Increased · Initiated · Instituted · Led · Mentored · Merged · Mobilized · Motivated · Navigated · Organized · Originated · Overhauled · Oversaw · Planned · Plotted · Predicted · Presided · Prioritized · Produced · Qualified · Recommended · Recognized · Recruited · Reviewed · Scheduled · Secured · Selected · Shaped · Streamlined · Stimulated · Strengthened · Supervised · Taught · Terminated · Trained · Unified.

**Gate:** Led, supervised and approved describe distinct responsibilities. A team contribution must not become sole leadership. Approved, certified and authorized require actual delegated authority.

## 6. Teaching, mentoring and learning

Adapted · Advised · Coached · Communicated · Conducted · Coordinated · Critiqued · Cultivated · Developed · Devoted · Educated · Evaluated · Explained · Facilitated · Focused · Fostered · Graded · Guided · Individualized · Informed · Inspired · Instilled · Instructed · Interacted · Mentored · Motivated · Persuaded · Simulated · Tested · Trained · Translated · Transmitted · Tutored.

**Gate:** A mentoring meeting is not a verified change in student outcomes. Keep selection, attendance, instructional delivery and measured learning separate.

## 7. Service, community, support and collaboration

Accompanied · Adapted · Adjusted · Advised · Advocated · Aided · Amended · Applied · Arranged · Assessed · Assisted · Attended · Coached · Collaborated · Contributed · Cooperated · Counseled · Demonstrated · Diagnosed · Educated · Enabled · Encouraged · Ensured · Expedited · Facilitated · Familiarized · Furthered · Guided · Partnered · Prepared · Prevented · Provided · Referred · Rehabilitated · Represented · Resolved · Served · Simplified · Staffed · Supplied · Supported · Volunteered.

**Gate:** Support and participation do not automatically demonstrate impact. Prefer actual activities and counts rather than unmeasured empowerment or transformation.

## 8. Achievements and outcomes

Attained · Completed · Demonstrated · Earned · Exceeded · Finished · Outperformed · Overcame · Reached · Showcased · Succeeded · Surpassed · Targeted · Won · Achieved · Acquired · Delivered · Gained · Generated · Increased · Produced · Reduced · Saved · Secured · Yielded.

**Gate:** Completed, selected, earned and won are not interchangeable. Outperformed requires a named comparator and metric. Be exact about multiple metrics that disagree.

## 9. Progress, improvement and change

Accelerated · Advanced · Amplified · Boosted · Capitalized · Centralized · Clarified · Conserved · Consolidated · Converted · Customized · Decreased · Delivered · Digitized · Enhanced · Expanded · Expedited · Furthered · Gained · Generated · Increased · Integrated · Lifted · Maximized · Merged · Modernized · Modified · Outpaced · Overhauled · Reconciled · Redesigned · Reduced · Refined · Refocused · Rehabilitated · Remodeled · Reorganized · Replaced · Restructured · Revamped · Revitalized · Saved · Simplified · Standardized · Stimulated · Streamlined · Strengthened · Sustained · Transformed · Updated · Upgraded.

**Gate:** Improvements need a versioned change or defensible before/after comparison. Refined means adjusted the method, not necessarily improved measured performance.

## 10. Building, engineering and maintenance

Assembled · Built · Conserved · Constructed · Converted · Designed · Determined · Developed · Engineered · Enhanced · Fabricated · Implemented · Improved · Inspected · Installed · Overhauled · Programmed · Regulated · Remodeled · Repaired · Restored · Serviced · Specialized · Specified · Standardized · Studied · Upgraded · Utilized.

**Gate:** A software prototype is not a deployed field installation. Serviced, installed or inspected require genuine relevant work. A model is not a certified equipment test.

## 11. High-risk verbs and evidence gates

| Verb | Necessary evidence | Prefer when not established |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Proved | Valid proof with premises, applicability and conclusion | derived, supported, tested |
| Validated | Appropriate independent physical or operational reference | evaluated, compared, numerically verified |
| Optimized | Objective, constraints, and verifiable optimum/bound | formulated, modeled, improved in simulation |
| Outperformed | Named competitor, metric, data and baseline | showed lower RMSE, improved a particular metric |
| Ensured / guaranteed | Controls and evidence exclude relevant failures in claimed range | checked, reduced risk, documented |
| Certified / approved | Formal authorized decision | evaluated, prepared, submitted for review |
| Deployed | Actual field or user operation | implemented, prototyped, developed |
| Discovered / first | Novelty search appropriate to claim | identified, investigated, proposed |
| Led / founded | Documented authority and individual role | contributed, coordinated, co-founded |
| Transformed | Broad verified attributable change | developed, introduced, extended |
| Solved | Defined problem solved and acceptance criteria met | analyzed, computed a candidate, formulated |
| Confirmed causality | Appropriate causal identification and physical mechanism evidence | observed association, proposed pathway |

## 12. Linguistic corrections to the source list

- **Advertising** is a gerund or activity noun. Use **advertised** when describing a completed action.
- **Remodel** is an infinitive/base form. For accomplished work, use **remodeled**.
- **Deducted** means subtracted or inferred, not reduced an economic cost. Use reduced only when actually measured.
- **Qualified** can concern professional status, evidence status or a defined operating domain. Name the object.
- Duplicate verbs in the original list are intentionally allowed across functional categories; they do not create separate achievements.

## 13. Recommended operational pattern

Before using any verb, ask:

1. What exactly was performed?
2. Who performed it and who else contributed?
3. What is the source of evidence?
4. Was the work proposed, formulated, executed, numerically checked, independently validated, or field-deployed?
5. What result and comparison can actually be documented?
6. What limitation would make the sentence misleading if omitted?

If an answer is missing, weaken the verb or state the uncertainty.
