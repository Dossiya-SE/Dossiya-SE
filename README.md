# Dossiya Dakou

### Financial Engineering | Sustainable Systems Engineering | Mathematical Modeling | Computational Decision Science

I am a graduate researcher and engineering practitioner working at the intersection of **quantitative finance**, **sustainable systems engineering**, and **data-driven modeling**.

My work focuses on building analytical and computational frameworks for complex systems shaped by uncertainty, constraints, feedback, and tradeoffs. I apply mathematical modeling, statistical reasoning, and systems thinking to problems in financial markets, environmental impact assessment, energy systems, waste systems, and sustainability decision support.
