# Contribution-Aware Scientific Reasoning

A reusable research protocol that combines **CRediT for documented human contributions** with **separate scientific claim verification**. These are distinct tracks.

> **Who contributed to the research?** and **What does the evidence establish?** require different answers.

## Start here

- [SKILL.md](SKILL.md): research reasoning cycle, seven scientific gates, claims and contribution protocols, thesis worked example, AI-assisted research.
- [CRediT_ROLE_MAP.md](CREDIT_ROLE_MAP.md): all 14 official role names with evidence examples and safeguards.
- [Scientific claims CSV](templates/SCIENTIFIC_CLAIMS_TEMPLATE.csv): blank scientific claim ledger.
- [Contributions CSV](templates/CONTRIBUTIONS_TEMPLATE.csv): blank attribution ledger. Do not publicly upload real names without consent.
- [Decision record](templates/RESEARCH_DECISION_RECORD.md): experiment-by-experiment scientific justification template.
- [Evidence-Led Writing Skill](../../writing/evidence-led-action-verb-writing/README.md): evidence-appropriate language after research results have been qualified.

## Scope

The protocol can help structure ASU MSE thesis research under existing SO1–SO3 objectives:

- SO1: qualify directional physical thermal mechanisms.
- SO2: formulate and test state-time physics and mathematics.
- SO3: examine supported transition, propagation and electricity/mobility service effects.

The source thesis repository and its frozen authority files were **not changed** by this module. No person is assigned a contribution role, and no scientific result is declared newly verified.

## Origin and standard boundary

Original CRediT taxonomy and implementation guidance: [CRediT official role descriptors](https://credit.niso.org/contributor-roles-defined/) and [implementation guidance](https://credit.niso.org/implementing-credit/).

The **seven-question scientific reasoning gate, scientific statuses and claim register are project-authored adaptations**, not NISO rules. They are intended for research planning and scientific evidence governance.

CRediT **does not determine authorship**. Extent terms like lead/equal/supporting are optionally used by publishers and are **not** currently official role taxonomy qualifiers.

## Applying in a research session

> Read research/contribution-aware-scientific-reasoning/SKILL.md and CREDIT_ROLE_MAP.md. For this specific hypothesis, help me populate a scientific claim and decision record without inventing a verification result or assigning unconfirmed contributions. Read my writing skill only when drafting a report.

## Privacy and reproducibility

Commit blank templates and approved public research explanations. Keep actual attribution records and unreleased data privately with appropriate permissions. Record AI assistance transparently under the applicable institution and publisher rules.
