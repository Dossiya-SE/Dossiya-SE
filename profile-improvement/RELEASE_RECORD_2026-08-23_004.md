## Design rationale

The page now separates three layers:

```text
identity / purpose
→ visible research architecture
→ deep technical detail on demand
```

This avoids making the first-pass profile behave like a full technical report while retaining the underlying mathematical depth.

## Scientific boundaries preserved

- cross-sector transferability remains a research programme, not a validated universal theory;
- mathematical art does not establish expertise or empirical validity;
- repository visibility does not imply evidence maturity;
- two master's programmes remain ongoing;
- unresolved technical and undergraduate credential titles remain under the existing release holds.
